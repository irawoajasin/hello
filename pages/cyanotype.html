cyanotypes as a form of critical data capture

What does it mean to capture the essence of the world around us in a spreadsheet, or in an array of pixels? What, if anything, can never be captured? Through this workshop we will wander through a collective understanding of what it means to capture representations of the urban environment through digital and analog images. Starting from NYC Open Data's most recent Street Tree Census, we will look at what the designers of the dataset deemed important, and what they left out. We will then visit the tree closest to the site of the workshop and collect metadata not captured in the NYC Open Dataset, and all take an image of the tree, distilling it into an array of pixels. Then we will delve deeper, gaining more intimate understandings of our image metadata through command line image analysis and manipulation. Through guided prompts, participants will then reflect on what it means to label image metadata through poetic embodiment through the creation of cyanotype images, also known as sun prints, about the tree.
