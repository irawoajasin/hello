extraordinary

(2026) Creative Direction, Graphic Design, Web Design

Creative direction for the 2025-2025 speaker series exploring creative practice on the margins. This series aimed to highlight the challenges of working outside of the box at all stages of the creative process: from conceptualization to production to curation to archival strategies. How do practitioners work with new methods and contexts to bring their extra-ordinary work to audiences?

The marketing approach included large scale screen graphic to engage visitors at various touchpoints of the 370 Jay Street building, as well as used vertical risograph printed flyers to extend the awareness of the programming beyond the screen and online engagement.
